Category strategy

Categories are the main browsing layer. Keep them broad enough to avoid clutter and specific enough to help users pick the right agent, skill, prompt system, knowledge base, or implementation pack.

  • Funding Ops should cover intake, triage, routing, documents, and follow-up.
  • Partner Enablement should cover affiliates, brokers, resource cards, and launch kits.
  • Content Ops should cover SEO, publishing workflows, repurposing, and briefs.
  • Automation should cover workflows, webhooks, CRM actions, review gates, and triggers.
  • Business Credit should stay educational, readiness-based, and non-promissory.
  • Asset Management should cover Vercel, GPTs, Gemini Gems, Flash UI, and portfolio inventory.
