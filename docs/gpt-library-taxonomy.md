# GPT Library Taxonomy

This document defines the category, visibility, brand-alignment, and status rules for the ChatGPT GPT Library Pack.

## Primary categories

Each GPT gets one primary category.

1. **Funding + Finance** — funding education, borrower triage, capital path planning, business loans, real estate capital, SaaS capital, and Moonshine Capital finance workflows.
2. **Business Credit / FinanceOS / BizCredit OS** — business credit, fundability, cash flow, business banking, and finance operating systems.
3. **Affiliate Partner Enablement** — broker onboarding, affiliate training, partner launch kits, referral systems, commissions, and disclosure support.
4. **AI Automation Agency** — AI consulting, GPT stacks, agent generation, automation services, and no-code delivery systems.
5. **Content, SEO, Blog + Media** — SEO, blogs, newsletters, podcasts, video repurposing, captions, and editorial workflows.
6. **Personal Brand / Thought Leadership** — Jason Feimster, Full-Time Hustler, Freedom Contrarian, Metamodernist Educator, essays, speaking, and personal brand systems.
7. **GPT Wrapper / SaaS Tool** — standalone GPT utilities, SaaS experiments, lead magnets, and productized tools.
8. **Web, Vercel + App Builder** — Vercel apps, static sites, Next.js helpers, landing pages, and engineering-as-marketing widgets.
9. **CRM, Ops + Automation** — HubSpot, Gmail parsing, n8n, intake workflows, pipeline ops, and back-office automation.
10. **Local Referral / Community** — local media, Kingstowne Digest, local referrals, events, and community activation.
11. **Experimental / Archive** — stale, duplicate, unclear, incomplete, or not-yet-promotable GPTs.

## Visibility levels

- `public` — safe for public directory, website, lead magnet, or marketplace listing.
- `partner-facing` — safe for approved partners, but not necessarily for public SEO.
- `internal-only` — contains sensitive routing, CRM, backend, funding, or operational logic.
- `archive` — stale, duplicate, unclear, or low-priority.

## Public safety rules

Public GPT pages must avoid private client data, secrets, API keys, backend-provider bypass paths, funding approval guarantees, and legal/tax/credit repair/financial advice claims.

## Internal-only triggers

Mark a GPT `internal-only` when it includes:

- broker routing logic
- borrower qualification logic
- internal funding matchmaking
- backend provider references
- CRM operations
- sensitive client intake workflows
- non-public Moonshine Capital operating logic
