# Public vs Internal GPT Rules

Use this document before promoting any Custom GPT outside the repo.

## Public GPTs

Public GPTs are safe for:

- website directories
- landing pages
- lead magnets
- public marketplace pages
- social sharing

Examples:

- BizCredit Builder GPT
- SiteForge Operator
- ZenSEO Article Writer
- AI Agent Generator

Public GPTs must avoid private data, internal routing, backend-provider bypass language, and funding guarantees.

## Partner-facing GPTs

Partner-facing GPTs are useful for approved affiliates, brokers, or referral partners but may not be ideal for public SEO pages.

Examples:

- Game Plan Wizard
- Fix & Flip Capital Coach

Use partner-facing when the GPT helps partners execute but may include operating assumptions, partner workflows, or broker enablement context.

## Internal-only GPTs

Internal-only GPTs should stay behind the curtain.

Use internal-only when a GPT includes:

- broker routing logic
- borrower qualification logic
- funding matchmaking
- CRM/API operations
- backend provider references
- sensitive intake workflows
- non-public Moonshine Capital operating rules

Examples:

- Funding Pathfinder
- Get My Funding Offer
- Harper — HubSpot API Architect

## Archive GPTs

Archive GPTs are stale, duplicate, unclear, experimental, or not ready for promotion.

Keep them for source history, but do not feature them publicly.

## Moonshine Capital safety notes

- Do not expose backend funding providers in public-facing docs unless explicitly approved.
- Do not create public slugs that expose backend providers.
- Do not promise approvals.
- Do not imply guaranteed funding.
- Do not provide legal, tax, credit repair, or financial advice.
- Keep internal routing logic separate from prospect-facing language.
