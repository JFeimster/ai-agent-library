Curated high-value agents, skills, prompt systems, and knowledge-base assets to build from first.

Featured criteria

Featured assets should be useful, complete enough to act on, relevant to a revenue or operating workflow, and connected to a clear next step.

  • The asset has a clear operator, buyer, or user.
  • The asset supports real execution, not just inspiration.
  • The asset can become a page, GPT, skill, workflow, schema, or template.
  • The asset is safe enough for public or semi-public positioning.

Recommended featured mix

  • 1–3 high-value agents.
  • 1–3 reusable skills.
  • 1–2 prompt systems.
  • 1–2 knowledge bases.
  • 1–2 portfolio or platform assets.
