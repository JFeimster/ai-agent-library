## Conversion types

- **Reusable agent spec:** Convert into a formal file under `agents/chatgpt-converted/`.
- **Lead magnet:** Package as a public tool, quiz, guide, or email capture asset.
- **Vercel tool:** Convert into a static/Next.js widget or calculator.
- **Internal copilot:** Keep behind the curtain for operations, CRM, funding routing, or partner management.
- **Partner-facing tool:** Share with approved partners after removing sensitive backend details.
- **Archive candidate:** Keep for reference but do not promote.

## Safety notes

Funding and CRM GPTs should not be made public until their public-facing language is reviewed for claims, provider exposure, privacy risk, and approval-guarantee issues.
