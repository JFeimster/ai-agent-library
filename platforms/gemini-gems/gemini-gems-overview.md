# Gemini Gems Overview

Gemini Gems in this repo are reusable Google Workspace-native assistants with saved instructions, repeatable context, and structured outputs.

## Best-fit workflows

- Google Docs drafting and SOP creation
- Google Sheets cleanup, formulas, and CRM normalization
- Gmail replies, follow-ups, and intake parsing
- Google Drive knowledge organization
- Google Slides pitch and training outlines
- Funding-readiness and partner enablement workflows

## Library role

Each Gem spec should be reusable as a source for:

- ChatGPT Custom GPTs
- Claude Projects
- n8n agents
- Vercel widgets
- Wix portal assistants
- Google Workspace workflows

## Operating principle

A Gem should have one clear job, one clear user, one repeatable output pattern, and enough guardrails to avoid becoming a confident little liability goblin.
