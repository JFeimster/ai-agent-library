# Funding + Finance GPTs

## Overview

GPTs in this category support funding education, capital path planning, borrower triage, real estate capital, SaaS funding, and Moonshine Capital-related finance workflows.

## Audience

- founders
- SMB owners
- funding advisors
- real estate investors
- SaaS founders
- approved funding partners
