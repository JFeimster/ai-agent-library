# Affiliate Disclosure Checker

## Purpose

The **Affiliate Disclosure Checker** skill reviews affiliate, referral, partner, sponsorship, funding, product-comparison, and monetized content for clear disclosure placement, public-safe claims, audience trust, and compliance-friendly language.

Use this skill when content includes affiliate links, referral links, broker links, partner CTAs, monetized recommendations, “best” lists, comparison pages, funding application links, lead forms, resource cards, or any situation where compensation may influence placement.

This skill helps keep the money machine honest without turning every page into a legal funeral program.

This skill does **not** provide legal advice, guarantee FTC compliance, approve regulated disclosures, or replace attorney/platform review.

## When To Use

- Sponsored content disclosure

## Disclosure Placement Rules

A disclosure should be:

- Clear
- Early
- Close to the relevant link or CTA
- Written in plain language
- Visible without hunting
- Repeated if the page is long
- Included before the user clicks a monetized link

Weak disclosure locations:

```text
footer only
buried terms page
tiny gray text
only after CTA
only on another page
hidden behind hover/click
```

Better disclosure locations:

```text
near intro
above comparison table
next to CTA
before first link
inside recommendation card
inside email before link
inside YouTube description before links
```

## Recommended Disclosure Language

### General Affiliate Disclosure

```text
Disclosure: Some links on this page may be affiliate or referral links. If you use them, we may earn compensation at no extra cost to you. We only include resources we believe may be useful, but you should compare options and decide what fits your situation.
```

### Funding / Finance Affiliate Disclosure

```text
Disclosure: Some funding or financial-service links may be referral or affiliate links. We may receive compensation if you submit information or become a customer through those links. This does not guarantee approval, funding, terms, rates, or outcomes.
```

### Comparison Page Disclosure

```text
Disclosure: Some providers listed here may compensate us through referral or affiliate relationships. Compensation may influence placement, but we aim to explain our criteria and present options in a useful, transparent way.
```

### Resource Card Disclosure

```text
Disclosure: This resource may include referral links. We may earn compensation if you use them, but approval, pricing, terms, and availability depend on the provider’s review.
```

### Email Disclosure

```text
Quick disclosure: this link may be a referral link, which means we may earn compensation if you use it. No approval or outcome is guaranteed.
```

### Social Caption Disclosure

```text
Disclosure: referral/affiliate link. No guaranteed results.
```

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Identify the Monetization Relationship

Classify as:

```text
affiliate
referral
sponsorship
paid placement
broker referral
lead generation
revenue share
owned product
unknown
```

If unknown, flag:

```text
Monetization relationship is unclear. Add disclosure if there is any chance compensation may influence the link or placement.
```

### Step 2 — Identify Content Type

Classify as:

```text
blog post
landing page
comparison page
directory page
tool page
resource card
email
social caption
YouTube description
newsletter
partner microsite
funding page
```

### Step 3 — Review Disclosure Presence

Check whether disclosure is:

```text
present
missing
too vague
too late
too hidden
not close to link
not platform-appropriate
```

### Step 4 — Review Placement

Recommended placement:

- Intro or top of page
- Before first affiliate link
- Near comparison tables
- Near primary CTA
- Repeated before final CTA if page is long

### Step 5 — Review Claims

Flag:

- Guaranteed outcomes
- Unsupported superlatives
- Missing qualification language
- Implied approval/funding/ranking certainty
- Unclear compensation influence
- Over-promising rates, terms, or speed

### Step 6 — Review CTAs

CTAs should match actual user action.

Good:

```text
Compare Ecommerce Funding Options
Run the Ad Spend Cash Flow Calculator
Check Funding Readiness
Explore Partner Resources
```

Weak/risky:

```text
Get Approved
Guarantee My Funding
Claim My Loan
```

### Step 7 — Produce Safer Rewrite

Rewrite only the risky pieces unless the user requests a full rewrite.

### Step 8 — Add Publish Checklist

Every review should end with a checklist.

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Blog / Website

- Disclosure should appear before first monetized link.
- Footer-only disclosure is not enough.
- Comparison pages should disclose ranking criteria.

### Email

- Disclosure should appear before or near the monetized link.
- Avoid hiding relationship in a footer only.

### YouTube

- Include disclosure verbally if the video makes recommendations.
- Include disclosure near the top of the description before links.

### Social Media

- Use clear disclosure such as `#ad`, `affiliate link`, or `referral link`.
- Avoid vague tags like `#partner` if the relationship is not obvious.

### Funding / Finance Pages

- Add approval-not-guaranteed language.
- Avoid implying specific rates, terms, or approval unless verified and conditional.
- Avoid credit repair framing unless legally reviewed.

## Guardrails

This skill must not:

- Claim legal compliance is guaranteed.
- Replace attorney review.
- Hide affiliate/referral relationships.
- Approve deceptive content.
- Encourage fake testimonials.
- Invent provider relationships.
- Invent compensation details.
- Invent rates, terms, or rankings.
- Guarantee funding, approval, revenue, or rankings.
- Recommend undisclosed paid placements.
