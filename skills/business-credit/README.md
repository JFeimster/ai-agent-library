# Business Credit Skills

Business credit skills support fundability diagnostics, utilization education, and readiness roadmaps.

## P0 skills

| Skill | File | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Fundability Diagnostic Skill | `fundability-diagnostic.skill.md` | Score business credit readiness |
| Utilization Math Skill | `utilization-math.skill.md` | Explain utilization and projected changes |
| Credit Readiness Roadmap Skill | `credit-readiness-roadmap.skill.md` | Generate 30/60/90-day readiness plan |

## Best app placements

- BizCredit OS widgets
- business credit landing pages
- readiness quizzes
- partner lead magnets

## Guardrails

- No credit repair advice.
- No score-increase guarantees.
- No funding approval guarantees.
