# Credit Readiness Roadmap Skill

```yaml
---
name: Credit Readiness Roadmap Skill
slug: credit-readiness-roadmap
category: business-credit
audience:
  - small business owners
  - founders
  - affiliate partners
  - business credit operators
problem_solved: Converts fundability gaps into a 30/60/90-day business credit readiness roadmap.
inputs_required:
  - fundability score
  - entity hygiene status
  - tradeline count
  - utilization estimate
  - banking status
  - business age
outputs:
  - 30-day priorities
  - 60-day priorities
  - 90-day priorities
  - required fixes
  - recommended CTA
guardrails:
  - Do not provide credit repair advice.
  - Do not guarantee approvals, score changes, or credit limits.
  - Use educational readiness language.
recommended_apps:
  - lead magnets
  - BizCredit OS
  - readiness quizzes
integration_notes:
  - Can consume Fundability Diagnostic outputs.
monetization_angle: Converts diagnostics into a paid audit, template, or managed service path.
---
```

## Purpose

Give users a practical phased roadmap instead of a vague “build business credit” sermon from the financial goblin cave.

## Example prompt

Create a 30/60/90-day business credit readiness roadmap from this fundability profile. Include priorities, blockers, and next CTA without giving credit repair advice.
