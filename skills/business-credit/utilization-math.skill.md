# Utilization Math Skill

```yaml
---
name: Utilization Math Skill
slug: utilization-math
category: business-credit
audience:
  - small business owners
  - founders
  - business credit operators
problem_solved: Calculates credit utilization and explains how paydowns or new limits can improve profile strength.
inputs_required:
  - total revolving balances
  - total revolving limits
  - planned paydown amount
  - proposed new limit if applicable
outputs:
  - current utilization
  - projected utilization
  - utilization band
  - plain-English explanation
  - next best action
guardrails:
  - Do not provide credit repair advice.
  - Do not guarantee score increases or funding outcomes.
  - Use educational language only.
recommended_apps:
  - business credit calculators
  - BizCredit OS widgets
  - readiness quizzes
integration_notes:
  - Can feed fundability diagnostics and credit readiness roadmaps.
monetization_angle: Makes business credit improvement easier to understand and act on.
---
```

## Purpose

Help users see how utilization affects fundability and what actions may improve their profile.

## Example prompt

Calculate current and projected utilization from these balances, limits, paydown, and new limit assumptions. Explain the impact in educational language.
