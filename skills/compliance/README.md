| Public Claims Review Skill | `public-claims-review.skill.md` | Flag risky public claims and safer rewrites |

- affiliate content workflows

## Guardrails

- Do not provide legal advice.
- Escalate high-risk claims for human review.
- Do not remove needed disclaimers for conversion convenience.
