# Commission Estimator Skill

```yaml
---
name: Commission Estimator Skill
slug: commission-estimator
category: partner-enablement
audience:
  - affiliate partners
  - funding brokers
  - referral partners
problem_solved: Calculates potential partner commissions from funding volume, close rate, deal size, and commission assumptions.
inputs_required:
  - target monthly income
  - average funded deal size
  - estimated close rate
  - commission percentage
  - number of leads or referrals
outputs:
  - estimated commission
  - required funded volume
  - required lead/referral activity
  - sensitivity notes
  - disclaimer language
guardrails:
  - Do not promise income.
  - Clearly label projections as estimates.
  - Do not imply guaranteed deal flow, approval, or funding.
recommended_apps:
  - partner recruiting pages
  - commission widgets
  - onboarding tools
integration_notes:
  - Can feed Partner Game Plan activity targets.
monetization_angle: Makes the partner opportunity concrete without turning it into cartoon money math.
---
```

## Purpose

Help prospective and active partners understand the activity and volume needed to hit income goals.

## Example prompt

Estimate partner commission potential using this target income, average deal size, close rate, and commission assumption. Include activity targets and disclaimers.
