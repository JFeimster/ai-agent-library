```yaml
---
name: Partner Profile Builder Skill
slug: partner-profile-builder

  - affiliate partners
  - funding brokers
  - referral partners

inputs_required:
  - partner name
  - background
  - niche
  - geography
  - audience served
  - preferred funding lanes
  - contact/CTA preference
outputs:
  - partner bio
  - positioning statement
  - niche summary
  - recommended resource stack
  - public-safe profile copy
guardrails:
  - Do not expose private contact details unless approved.
  - Do not overstate credentials.
  - Do not imply the partner is a lender unless they are licensed/approved.

  - partner directory
```

## Purpose

Create credible partner profiles without making every new broker stare at a blank page like it owes them money.

## Example prompt

Build a public-safe partner profile from this intake. Include bio, niche, audience, CTA, and recommended resource cards.
