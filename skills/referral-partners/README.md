# Referral Partner Skills

Referral partner skills support CPAs, business brokers, consultants, and strategic partners who can send qualified business owners into the right funnel.

## P0 skills

| Skill | File | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| CPA Referral Opportunity Skill | `cpa-referral-opportunity.skill.md` | Identify CPA client referral opportunities |
| Business Broker Deal Funding Skill | `business-broker-deal-funding.skill.md` | Identify acquisition and post-close funding needs |
| Referral Partner Pitch Skill | `referral-partner-pitch.skill.md` | Generate outreach and partner recruitment copy |
| Referral Source Attribution Skill | `referral-source-attribution.skill.md` | Preserve referral, UTM, and partner source data |

## Best app placements

- CPA landing pages
- business broker pages
- strategic partner funnels
- referral tracking flows

## Guardrails

- No referral income guarantees.
- No client funding guarantees.
- No tax, legal, investment, or financial advice.
