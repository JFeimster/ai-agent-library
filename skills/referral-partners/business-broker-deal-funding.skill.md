## Purpose

Help business brokers and M&A advisors spot funding gaps early and route buyers into a better-prepared capital conversation.

## Example prompt

Analyze this business acquisition scenario for likely capital gaps, buyer readiness issues, and recommended next steps. Keep the language broker-safe and do not guarantee financing.
