## Purpose

Give CPAs and bookkeepers a clean, professional way to spot clients who may benefit from capital readiness or funding conversations.

## Example prompt

Review this CPA client scenario and identify whether there is a capital, cash-flow, or business credit referral opportunity. Generate client-safe talking points and an intro email.
